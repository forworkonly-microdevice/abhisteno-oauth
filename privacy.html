GrassStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GrassStudio, developed by ABHIJNA MICRO (OPC) PVT. LTD., handles your information.

2. Data stored on your device

Your notes, settings and account details are stored locally on your device. Imported images and videos are kept in the application's data folder.

3. Third-party services

When you use AI, voice, image, document-conversion or cloud-storage features, the text, audio, image or file you choose is sent to the third-party service for that feature so it can process your request. You are asked before each service is used for the first time, and you can withdraw permission anytime in Settings.

4. API keys

API keys you add are kept on your device and are only sent to the provider they belong to.

5. Data we do not collect

GrassStudio does not sell your personal data and does not use advertising trackers.

6. Your choices

You can delete your notes at any time, remove saved API keys, and revoke permission for any third-party service in Settings.

7. Children

GrassStudio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the app will ask you to review and accept the new version.
